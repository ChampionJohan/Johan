## This Is the Third

| Book | What it looks at |
|---|---|
| One · The One Who Was Already There | What had arrived before I ever asked |
| Two · The One Who Never Asked | Going toward what will not come back |
| **Three · The One Who Stayed** | What continues even when they do not know |

You can read this one alone. The three are one line drawn in three parts, so
anyone who has read the first two will see more here. Set the three covers side
by side and the line joins.

## One Thing This Book Does Differently

In the first two books I kept setting out to explain something and stopping. The
stopping places are recorded at the end of each chapter.

This is the book where those stopping places gather. So **there are fewer
answers here than in the first two.** What there is instead is the things that
do not end. What continues when the other person cannot recognize you, what waits
for what is not coming back, what remains after the person is gone.

## Every Chapter Ends With a Box

> ## Someone Comes to Mind

Not something to do. Someone to remember. This book does not tell you to do
anything. It points at what has already happened, and that happened to you, not
to me. So that box is one I cannot fill.

## There Are Three Painful Chapters

The first, the seventh and the tenth. One more than in either of the other books.

To look at what does not end you have to look at what does, and the ending place
hurts. **A book that does not hurt is not a warm book.** You may shut it part
way. Shutting it is also a way of reading.

## And This Book Does Not Finish Either

It is the last chapter of the three and no knot is tied there.

That is not an oversight. In the last chapter I ask one question and write no
answer. The question kept rising through all three books, and I have no standing
to write the answer, so I write only the question. **The answer is not mine to
give, and there is not one line after that chapter.**

## How the Facts Are Handled

Each chapter ends with Where This Chapter Came From, holding both what I checked
and what I cut because I could not check it. There is almost no outside material
here either. Put a number in and the people in this book become statistics.
Anything unverified was cut whole rather than softened.
