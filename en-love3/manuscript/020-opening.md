## Opening

Ending is normal. That is the first thing to set down.

What is between people mostly ends. You drift from someone you were close to,
you stop seeing someone you saw daily, and a feeling that seemed capable of
anything cools in a few years. Nothing is wrong in that and nobody is deficient
for it. Ending is the natural direction, and for something not to end, something
has to go in.

What this book looks at is the side that does not end. **Places where nothing
comes back and nothing stops.**

Someone sits for years beside a person who cannot recognize them. Someone spends
a lifetime waiting for what may never come. Someone holds on while hating, and
someone still uses the turns of phrase of a person who is no longer in the world.
By the arithmetic it does not explain. The second book spent twelve chapters
watching that arithmetic fail and ended one explanation short.

I will not claim to fill that gap here. I cannot. What I can do is gather the
things that do not end and set them in one place. Twelve chapters, and one
question follows all twelve.

> Every reason to end is present. Why does it not end.

In the last chapter I ask that question once more in a different form. And stop
there.
