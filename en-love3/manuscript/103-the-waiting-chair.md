## While Waiting You Appear to Be Doing Nothing

There are chairs in a corridor. The person sitting in one is doing nothing. That
is how it looks from outside.

Outside an operating room, outside an emergency department, outside an
examination room, in an arrivals hall. The person sitting there is holding nothing and has nothing to do. They are there for hours. Asked to write
down what they did in that time, there is nothing to write.

And that time is the longest time that person has. And anyone who has sat in that
chair **knows that sitting there is the work.**

## The Waiting Side Has No Power

Waiting has one property. The one waiting can do nothing.

Inside the operating room other people are working. The waiting side cannot get
into that work, cannot make it go better, cannot make it end sooner. There is
exactly one thing available to them. Being in that place.

That looks like nothing and it is not, and when it is over and the other comes
out, it matters enormously whether anyone was in that chair, and the one
coming out remembers it for the rest of their life. One thing happened in
those hours, and the one thing lasts for decades.

## Waiting for What May Not Come

There is a longer kind of waiting. The kind with no fixed day on which it ends.

Waiting for someone who left to come back is like that. Waiting for a child who
stopped answering. Waiting for someone whose fate is unknown. This kind is not
hours but decades. And unlike the operating room, there is no door. With no door
to open, it does not end.

Look at people in that position and the waiting has become their life. They eat
and work and laugh, and underneath it the waiting is laid down. A phone rings and
the thought comes once. Decades on, it still comes once.

## Is Waiting Love

Something catches here. Whether it is right to call waiting love.

Waiting can have holding-on mixed into it. Being unable to let go and loving look
the same from outside. Wait for someone who does not want to come back and the
waiting becomes a burden on them. So waiting is not always good.

I have thought about where the two divide. One thing occurred to me. **Whether
the waiting side presents the waiting as a bill.** Say *I waited this long* and
it is an invoice. Wait and say nothing and it is not.

That line is not clean either. There are cases where the other person knows
without being told, and knowing is also a weight. I cannot draw it.

## Someone Comes to Mind

Was there someone sitting there when you came out of somewhere. You do not know
how many hours they sat. You may remember that there was nothing in their hands.

## Where This Chapter Came From

The corridor in this chapter is a general scene and not a particular place.

There is material on families waiting for relatives who went missing. I did not
use it. Using their waiting to make the argument of this chapter would turn that
waiting into my raw material. I decided against it.
