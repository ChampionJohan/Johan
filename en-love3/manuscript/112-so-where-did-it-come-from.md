## What the Three Books Looked At

Set the three books in a line and it goes like this.

The first looked at the fact that we started out having received. Things never
asked for and never paid for had arrived first. The second looked at what was
received going sideways. It goes where nothing comes back, and it goes although
the arithmetic does not work. The third looked at it not ending. It goes when the
other person cannot know, it waits when nothing returns, it stays after the
person is gone.

Thirty-six chapters later, one thing is left. **What happened can be confirmed,
and why it happens cannot.**

## Every Jam Was the Same Jam

I gathered up the places where I got stuck across the three books. Gathered up,
they were not different places.

I did not know where the strength to continue at a loss comes from, and I
could not explain the people who received nothing and give a great deal. I
could not find the reason for sitting for years beside someone who cannot
recognize you. I could not find how the counting stops. Four times I built an
explanation and four times I discarded it.

The discarded explanations had something in common. All of them looked for the
answer **inside the person.** Will, character, reward, circumstance, what was
received. Every time I tried to explain it with something inside a person it came
up short at some point. And the amount it came up short by was not small.

## Is This a Quantity a Person Can Produce

Let me measure one thing here. Whether what is in this book is a quantity a human
being can produce.

Giving way ten times is something a person does. A hundred, probably. And going
every day for seven years to someone who cannot recognize you. Sending for thirty
years to a child who does not answer. Going a whole life toward where absolutely
nothing comes back. The quantity does not match what can be explained by human
strength.

And people like that are not rare. If they were rare I would call them
exceptions. They are common. There is one in every neighborhood, and there was
one in nearly every family. **That something inexplicable is extremely common**
is the strangest fact I met while writing these three books.

## Where Did the Chain Start

Look again at the explanation from the first book. We pass it on because we
received, it said. That explanation fits most cases.

Push it backward, though, and it does not end. What I received came from my
mother. She received it from her mother. She received it from hers. Keep going and
the chain started somewhere, and whoever stood at the start **must have given
without having received.**

Giving without having received. The place that jammed four times across three
books is standing there at the start of the chain as well, and the same thing
has to have happened at the end of the line. So this chain does not look as
though it started with people. **What people passed between themselves does
not account for the beginning.**

## This Is as Far as I Looked

I would like to go further. Go further and it stops being what I saw and
becomes what I believe. Write what you believe as though you saw it and all
three of these books collapse, and the eleventh chapter of the second book
said an explanation that cannot be argued against is not an explanation, and I
will keep that.

So I stop here. What I can write down ends here.

> Sometimes a person gives more than they were given.
> That much more did not come from a person.

## Where This Chapter Came From

This chapter alone has its order changed. The sources come first and Someone
Comes to Mind goes after. That box had to be the last box in the book.

Nothing in this chapter was brought in from outside. It stands on what the
preceding thirty-five chapters brought.

The near-absence of outside material across these three books was deliberate
from the start, and there were several places where a number could have
propped something up and all of them came out. What was left out is recorded
at the end of each chapter. Few books record what they did not put in, and I
think it is the more useful of the two.

And there is one thing I did not write in this chapter. I did not write the
answer. Not because I do not have one, but because this is not my place to write
it. Written here it becomes my answer, and this is a question the reader has to
take as their own. So the question is left and the book closes.

## Someone Comes to Mind

One person has kept coming to mind across these three books.

They gave you more than they were given. There was no reason for it. Not because
you were good, not because they had something to collect, not because anybody
told them to. They still could not explain it today.

What that person gave you was more than what they had.

> So where did it come from.
