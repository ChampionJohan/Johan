## Only One Side Is Left

What is between people has two sides, and one day only one side is left.

There is a side that stopped answering and a side that keeps sending, and a
side that does not call at the holiday and a side that calls anyway. A side
that left the house and a side that leaves the door unlocked, and from one
side that relationship is over, and from the other it is not.

This chapter stands on the side where it is not.

## There Are Reasons For Not Coming Back

Let me not write badly of the side that does not come back. That would ruin the
chapter.

There are usually reasons. Somebody was hurt in that house. Somebody knows
that going back means being hurt again, and for some people distance is the
only way of keeping themselves intact, and telling them to go back does harm.

So this is not a chapter telling anyone to come back. It looks only at **what
happens on the waiting side**, with the not-coming-back side left alone.

## The Sending Side's Years

Watch people who keep sending to someone who does not answer and they pass
through several stages.

First comes anger. Then comes looking for the reason. What did I do wrong, was it
that thing. After a few years they stop looking for the reason. And then comes
the odd place, where **the anger drops out and only the sending is left.**

People who have reached that stage use similar sentences. They say they send
even though it is not answered. They say they do not even expect a reply. And
they send every time. How it works to send while expecting nothing I cannot
explain.

## They Do Not Declare an End

One observation to record. Those people **never declare an end.**

They do not say that is enough, no more. Perhaps because they know saying it is
what makes it stop. Perhaps because the sentence simply will not come out of
their mouth. Without the declaration the relationship stays open. It stays open
with nothing passing through it, for decades.

So if the other side comes back one day, the place is still there. The one coming
back is startled by that. The place is sitting there as though nothing happened
in the meantime. Nothing did not happen. Someone kept that place empty for
decades.

## And If They Do Not Come Back

Here is the stuck place. Not coming back is the more common case.

Often the one waiting goes first. Then the waiting ends unused. Nothing happened
and nobody knows. What to call those decades I do not know.

I would like to write that it was not wasted, and I do not have standing to
write it. I was not the one waiting, and if the person who did the waiting
says it, it is their sentence; written by me, it is a performance of comfort.
So that sentence is out and the chapter stands without it.

## Someone Comes to Mind

Is there someone you do not contact. They may still be keeping your place empty.
I will not tell you to go back. If you have your reasons not to, those reasons
are right too.

## Where This Chapter Came From

Nothing in this chapter was brought in from outside. Observation and things I was
told, with no names, because they were about families.

There is material on why distance from family is sometimes necessary. I started
reading further in that direction and cut it as outside the scope of this
chapter. That it exists is worth recording, because some readers will finish this
chapter feeling they got it wrong. They did not.
