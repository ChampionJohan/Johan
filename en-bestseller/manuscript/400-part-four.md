## Where time appears to run backwards

Three chapters. And the most consoling part of this book.

**Which is why it has to be the most accurate.**

The things here didn't sell at first.
They looked like they'd soon be gone, and for a while they effectively were.

And they're here now.

## The format changes

Parts Two and Three were autopsies. One object, opened up.

Part Four is a **chronicle.** It follows the time.

```
made → didn't sell → what happened meanwhile →
where the direction turned → now
```

The middle box is the work of this part.

**What kept it alive during the years it wasn't selling.**

## It splits three ways

Three ways of arriving late, sorted by what changed.

| Chapter | What changed | The object was |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | **The world** changed and a slot appeared | Unchanged |
| 12 | **The use** changed and it moved | Unchanged |
| 13 | **Nothing changed.** People kept it | Unchanged |

The right-hand column is the same in all three.

**The object didn't change from beginning to end.**
So what's to be learned here isn't how to fix an object.

## Two risks in this part

**One. The survivor problem is worst here.**

The unnamed box from Chapter 4 has to come out again.
The things that simply vanished without selling are overwhelmingly more numerous.

What's here is known to us **because it survived.**
**There's no list of the ones that died under the same conditions.**

So the lesson of this part is **not "hold on and it'll come."**

The question is a different one.

> **What bought it the time?**

**Two. This territory has more stories than facts.**

Dramatic anecdotes attach themselves to things discovered late.
And those anecdotes usually have no source.

This book's rule is applied most strictly here.

> **Widely known does not mean true.**

Unverified anecdotes were **cut whole** rather than softened.
How many were cut is recorded in *How This Book Was Made.*
