## Why Is This Still Here?

### How Things Go from Selling Out to Never Leaving

---

The two words in the original title sat side by side on purpose:
**bestseller** and **steady seller**.

Putting them next to each other is the argument.

**They are not a bigger and a smaller version of the same success.
They are different work.**

> **A bestseller bets on what people are talking about.
> A steady seller bets on when people get stuck.**

This book measures books, clothes, appliances, food, films and music
with the same ruler. A ruler where thermal underwear and a book on
raising a child land in the same column.

And at the end it builds a bridge.

**Most things that lasted were not built to last.
They changed lanes at some point.**

Where that change happens is what this book is trying to teach.
