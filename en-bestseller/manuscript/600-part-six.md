## From here it's short

Five chapters, and the shortest in the book.

There's a reason.

**A checklist is not an explanation.**

The explaining was done by the previous five parts.
Explaining again here adds length and no use.

## Two rules

**One. No new concepts.**

If a word appears for the first time in Part Six,
it means the previous five parts were thin.
Only what has already appeared gets used here.

The repetition isn't hidden.
**A sentence that appears three times means it's the only thing this book actually says.**

**Two. No names.**

Same as Part One. No company names, no product names.

Parts Two through Five needed names. They were evidence.
Part Six is not a place for evidence. **It's a place for listing the work.**

And Part One and Part Six have to age more slowly than anything else here.

## The order

| Chapter | What it does |
|---|---|
| 16 | **Read** — what is blocked right now |
| 17 | **Break out** — make a reason to buy today |
| 18 | **Land** — lay the four bridges in order |
| 19 | **Look far** — sit on what won't move |
| 20 | **Who has the advantage** — this book's answer |

Chapter 17 comes before 18 because that's the order most people do it in.

**The argument of this part is to do them at the same time.**

As Chapter 7 showed, bridges are only cheap while the talk is alive.
Think about landing after breaking out and it's already expensive.

**Please read 17 and 18 together.**
