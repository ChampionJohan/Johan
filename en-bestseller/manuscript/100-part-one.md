## Starting without names

Four chapters.

Every tool this book uses comes out here.
Three clocks, four bridges, and four paths.

**Part One contains no company names and no product names.**

That's deliberate. Names age.

A name everybody knows today will need explaining in ten years,
and an example that needs explaining slows an argument instead of helping it.

**Part One has to age more slowly than anything else in this book.**

There's also no sources section in Part One. There are no numbers to check.
Everything said here is about structure,
and whether the structure holds gets tested by names starting in Part Two.

## The order

| Chapter | The question |
|---|---|
| 1 | What is different about the two successes? |
| 2 | Different clocks run on different objects |
| 3 | The customers never come back. Why is it still open? |
| 4 | How many routes are there between staying and vanishing? |

Chapter 1 separates the two. Chapter 2 builds the ruler that separates them.
Chapter 3 shows how the lasting side actually works.
Chapter 4 opens the map.

**After four chapters you can pick up any object and describe it in this book's words.**
The remaining five parts are the process of checking whether those words hold.
