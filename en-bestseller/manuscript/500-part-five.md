## The path that doesn't fit the table

Two chapters. The shortest part of this book,
and the center of gravity is here.

Bring back the table from Chapter 4.

The two questions were whether it broke out and whether it's still here.
This path doesn't fit into the four boxes they make.

**Something that sat quietly for a long time and suddenly broke out.**

## What's different from the third path

It looks like the late arrivals of Part Four. One thing is decisive.

| | ③ Arrived late | ④ Sat, then broke out |
|---|---|---|
| Meanwhile | Forgotten | **Still selling** |
| The maker | Usually gone | **Alive** |
| Who collects | Someone else | **The maker** |

Part Four said it: for the late arrivals the maker was often not there.
When the world came, there was nobody to receive it.

**Only on this path does the maker collect.**

Which makes it the only path with practical meaning for someone making something.

## A request about reading order

The two chapters are opposite in nature.

**Chapter 14 looks at what happens.** It's the hopeful one.
**Chapter 15 looks at why most can't catch it.** It's the conditions one.

One request.

**Read them in one sitting.**

Read Chapter 14 alone and this part becomes comfort.
And comfort makes a person feel better and then changes nothing.

Chapter 15 is where the work comes from.

## The limit of this part

**This is the part of the book that is hardest to verify.**

When something broke out is confirmable in records.
Charts, revenue, visitor counts.
**Why it broke out is not confirmable.**

Afterwards the explanations are abundant.
Articles appear, analyses appear, reasons get organised.

**Those explanations are almost all attached afterwards.**

Write a retrofitted explanation as a cause and this part becomes fiction.

So in this part it's written this way.

> **What happened is recorded. That it was the cause is not.**

For a reader that can be frustrating.
**The frustration is what accuracy costs.**
