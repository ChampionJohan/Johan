## Anatomy of Money for Teens

### How Businesses Really Make Money — and How You Can Start

**The Five Boxes Series · Teen Edition, Book One**

---

**Is there really such a thing as a head for money?**

There is. But nobody's born with it.

It shows up in people who know five questions.
That's the whole difference — some people know them, some people don't.

> **1. Who actually pays?**
> **2. What are they paying for?**
> **3. How and when does the money arrive?**
> **4. Why hasn't anyone taken this yet?**
> **5. Where does it break?**

In this book we call those five questions **the Five Boxes.**

Part One teaches you the boxes, one at a time.
Part Two puts ten brands you already know through all five.
Part Three is about what you could actually start.

By the end, the companies in the news and the apps on your phone
will all be things you can take apart.
