RoamRate Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

RoamRate ("the app") is a currency converter and travel expense tracker. This page explains what data the app handles and how.

What data the app stores

  • Expense entries you add: date, category, currency, amount, and an optional memo.
  • Exchange rate and budget settings you configure.
  • Feedback you choose to submit through the "Send Feedback" form, plus an email address only if you type one in (used solely to reply to you).
  • Anonymous usage counters — how many times the app was opened and how many entries were added, in aggregate. No device or personal identifiers are attached to these counts.

Where it's stored

By default, all data stays only on your device (browser local storage) — it never leaves your phone or computer. If you turn on "Sync across devices" with a sync code, your entries and settings are stored in a Google Firebase/Firestore database operated by the app's developer, grouped under the sync code you chose. No account, name, or login is required to use sync — anyone who knows your sync code can read and write that data, so treat it like a password.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. This version of the app does not include third-party analytics SDKs or ad trackers.

Children

RoamRate is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
