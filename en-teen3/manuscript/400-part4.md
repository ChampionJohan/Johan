Part Two was the ones that vanished. Part Three was the ones that stayed.

Part Four is something else.

**Things that failed once and succeeded later.**

Here's what matters.

These were **not remade.**
Not fixed, not relaunched.

**The object stayed the same and only the result changed.**

Which leaves one question.

> **If the object didn't change and the result did,
> the result wasn't inside the object.**

So where was it?

Part Four splits that across three chapters.

| Chapter | What changed |
|---|---|
| 10 | **the outside** — the world arrived late |
| 11 | **where it got used** — the use changed |
| 12 | **who was looking** — the old thing became new |

All three say the same thing.

**Not selling doesn't have to mean it was bad.**

Used wrongly that sentence is genuinely dangerous,
so the front of Chapter 10 is given over entirely to that.
