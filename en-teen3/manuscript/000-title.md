## Everyone Had One. Where Did They All Go?

### Why Fads End, and Why Some Things Don't

**The Teen Edition of *Why Is This Still Here?***

---

Last year there was something everybody in your class had.

The name will be different for each of you.
A keyring, a card, an app, a phrase everyone said.

For a while, not having one made you the odd one out.

**Nobody has one now.**

There's no date it ended. Nobody called a meeting and voted to stop.
At some point everyone just wasn't doing it anymore.

This book starts there.

But the thing it actually wants to talk about is the other side.

**Some things in that same room have been there for years.**

Nobody calls them a trend. Everybody keeps using them.
You'd notice if they were gone.

> **Those are two different things.
> And you can learn to tell them apart.**

This book is about learning that.
