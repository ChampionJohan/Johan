## These were fads too

Three chapters. The most important part of this book.

One thing to make clear first.

**Everything in Part Three started where everything in Part Two started.**

They were enormous fads. Everyone talked about them, queued for them,
felt behind without one.

**You could not tell them apart from the Part Two things.**
Nobody knew then which side would stay.

So Part Three asks one question:

> **They started in the same place. Where did they split?**

## One rule — traits Part Two also had don't count

Looking at survivors creates a problem. **Too many reasons appear.**

Good product. People loved it. Luck. The maker meant it.
All true, all useless.

**Every Part Two thing had all of those.**

So there's a rule in this part:

> **A trait that Part Two also had does not count as a reason.**

Apply it and very little survives.
What survives is the actual answer.

## The four bridges get used here

The four named in Chapter 2 come out now.

| Chapter | Bridge built | What it asks |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | **Patina · Handoff** | does it improve with use? can it pass on? |
| 8 | **Shape** | is it recognized without explanation? |
| 9 | **Repeat** | does the moment come round and get bought again? |

Two are in Chapter 7 for a reason.

Patina and Handoff are **landing bridges.**
They decide where a thing sits once it comes down off the fad.

Shape and Repeat are **the bridges that hold the seat.**

## And the limit

Part Three only looks at survivors.

Things built the same bridges and vanished anyway.
**There must have been. They just aren't in the record.**

This book isn't saying "do this and you'll last."
It's saying **"the ones that lasted turn out to have these things on them."**

Read it with that difference in mind.
