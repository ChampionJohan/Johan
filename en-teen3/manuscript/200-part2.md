## Why start with the dead ones

Three chapters. This is where names first appear in this book.

Starting with survivors feels good. You learn less.

Line up only the survivors and **too many things look shared.**
It was a good product, they got lucky, people loved it.
All true, all useless.

Start with the ones that vanished and it changes.

**Those were good products too, and people loved them too.**

> **A trait the dead ones also had can't be the reason the live one lived.**

Part Two does that filtering.
When Part Three looks at survivors, the already-filtered traits don't count.

## Same order each time

```
when it broke out → what was selling → when it turned → what got cut first
```

The last box is the one that matters.

When something collapses, several things collapse at once and the cause hides.
**Finding the one thing that got cut first is the job of this part.**

## Three ways to die

| Chapter | What got cut first | The product was |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | **the talk** — the conversation ended | fine |
| 5 | **the spot** — nowhere left to use it | actually improving |
| 6 | **the scarcity** — they threw it away themselves | selling well |

In all three, nothing was wrong with the product.

**Which means making a better product stops none of them.**
