## Three chapters

Every tool this book uses comes out here.
**Three clocks and four bridges.** That's all of it.

Part One has no company names and no product names.
That's on purpose. **Names age.**

A name everybody knows now will need explaining in a few years,
and an example that needs explaining slows you down instead of helping.

Names start in Part Two. They're needed there.

## The order

| Chapter | What it asks |
|---|---|
| 1 | What's the difference between a trend and a thing people keep using? |
| 2 | Why do some things get old fast and some never do? |
| 3 | Why doesn't a shop die when customers don't come back? |

Chapter 1 splits the two. Chapter 2 builds the ruler that splits them.
Chapter 3 shows how the lasting side actually runs.

**After three chapters you can pick up any object and explain it
in this book's words.**
