# One thing to confirm first

**KDP probably will not take a Korean paperback.**

KDP supports different language sets for ebooks and for print.
Korean is supported for Kindle ebooks. **The print list is much shorter.**

Several sources give the print languages as English, French, German, Spanish,
Italian, Portuguese and Japanese among others, and **say Korean is not on it.**

**I did not confirm this on KDP's own page.** That page could not be reached
from the working environment.

> **Open KDP Help → "Book Supported Languages" and check it yourself.**

So plan it this way.

| | Ebook | Paperback |
|---|---|---|
| **English edition** | KDP | KDP |
| **Korean edition** | KDP | **A domestic Korean printer** |

If the Korean paperback goes to a domestic service, its cover has to be rebuilt
to that specification. **The interior PDF already exists at A5.**

# 2 · Description — ready to paste

```
The shop with the queue last year is gone. The old one at the end of the same
street is still there. Which was better at business?

That the question is wrong is where this book starts.

A dessert franchise opened its five hundredth store two years after it began.
Seven months later there were four hundred and ninety. On the approach to a
shrine in Kyoto, a shop has sold one grilled rice cake for a thousand years
and is now run by the twenty-fifth generation. Its stated policy is not to
make the business bigger.

They were not better and worse at the same work. They were doing different
work. One sold what people were talking about. The other sold what people
keep needing. Talk ends. Need doesn't.

This book measures books, clothes, appliances, food, film and music with the
same ruler — a ruler where thermal underwear and a book on raising a child
land in the same column. It builds three clocks to tell you where a thing is
now, four bridges to tell you where it can go, and four paths to tell you what
has happened to things like it before.

Then it does the part most books skip. It examines what died, first, so that
the traits the dead ones also had can be struck off the list of reasons the
others lived.

What it arrives at is uncomfortable and useful. Most things that lasted were
not planned. They just never disappeared. Which makes this a game that favours
whoever started late — because staying is counted from today, and whoever
started first also goes to zero if they quit.

Twenty chapters, each ending with one line to write down today.
```

# 4 · Categories — three

KDP's category tree changes often. Rather than give exact paths that may no
longer exist, here is **which area to aim at.** Pick the closest match in the
browser.

| Rank | Aim at |
|---|---|
| 1 | Business & Money → **Marketing & Sales** |
| 2 | Business & Money → **Small Business & Entrepreneurship** |
| 3 | Business & Money → **Industries → Retailing** or **Consumer Behavior** |

**Narrow beats broad.** Fifth in a small category beats hundredth in a large one.

# 5 · Content — the AI question is here

**Answer this honestly.**

KDP has asked about AI use since 2023, and the line is clear.

| | Meaning | Disclose? |
|---|---|---|
| **AI-generated** | An AI tool **created** it. **Still counts even if you edited heavily** | **Yes** |
| **AI-assisted** | You created it; AI only refined or checked it | No |

Text, images and **translations** all count.

**This book is all three.**

| Item | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Text | **Yes, AI-generated** | I wrote the manuscript |
| Images | **Yes, AI-generated** | I made the cover |
| Translation | **Yes, AI-generated** | I produced the English edition |

Answer yes to all three, and if asked for extent, answer **all of it.**

**This answer is for Amazon's records. It is not shown on the product page.**
So there is no sales reason to answer otherwise.

Answering wrongly, on the other hand, **breaks KDP's terms.**
If the account is actioned, every book on it comes down. There is no upside.

# 6 · Pricing

## Ebook

The 70% band is fixed.

> **$2.99 to $9.99 earns 70%. Anything outside earns 35%.**

At 70% a delivery fee comes off, by file size ($0.15/MB in the US).
This EPUB is under 1 MB, so **about ten cents.**

| List | Rate | Roughly |
|---|---|---|
| $9.99 | 70% | **about $6.90** |
| $6.99 | 70% | about $4.80 |
| $4.99 | 70% | about $3.40 |
| $10.99 | **35%** | about $3.85 |

**$10.99 pays less than $6.99.** Crossing the band halves the rate.

> **Recommended: $9.99**

## Paperback — 333 pages

Printing cost comes from the page count. `$1.00 + (pages × $0.012)`

```
1.00 + (333 × 0.012) = $5.00
```

And on Amazon.com a paperback earns **60% only at $9.99 or more.** Below that, 50%.

| List | Rate | Calculation | Royalty |
|---|---|---|---|
| **$14.99** | 60% | 8.994 − 5.00 | **about $3.99** |
| $12.99 | 60% | 7.794 − 5.00 | about $2.79 |
| $9.99 | 60% | 5.994 − 5.00 | about $0.99 |

**On a 333-page book, cutting the price leaves almost nothing.**
Printing is $5, so $9.99 returns under a dollar.

> **Recommended: $14.99**

## Leave Expanded Distribution off

The rate drops to 40%, and there is no bookstore channel to serve yet.
It can be switched on at any time later.

## Other marketplaces

**Leave them alone.** They convert from the US price automatically.

# 7 · Order

```
1. English ebook       →  first. It is the simplest
2. English paperback   →  a separate product from the ebook
3. Korean ebook        →  KDP
4. Korean paperback    →  domestic, after checking KDP's language support
```

Ebook first because **print review takes longer and gets rejected more often.**
Better to learn the account and the process on an ebook.
