## Nobody Chose It

Of everything that stays attached to a person for life, there is one thing
**the person did not pick.** The name. A name is settled before the one who
will carry it arrives, sometimes months before, sometimes earlier still and
then held in waiting, and it is made at a time when nobody knows what sort of
person this will be.

So a name carries no information about its owner. What a name holds is **the
mind of the one who made it.**

## There Is a Wish Inside

Watch people choosing a name and they all do roughly the same things. They look
for a good meaning. They say it aloud to hear how it sits. They wonder whether
it will get the child teased. And they hesitate for a long time. Days. Weeks,
in a lot of houses.

What is inside that hesitation is a wish. How they would like this child to
turn out. **A wish laid in advance on someone who has not yet done anything.**
And the wish has no condition on it. No household says do this and then you get
the name. It is given first.

## Every Time It Is Called, the Wish Goes By

There is something a name does that nobody notices. Every time it is spoken,
the wish inside it passes through once more. The speaker almost never knows.
They are calling someone to dinner, asking where they are going. And the sound
carries the whole of what was put into it at the start.

Which is why names do not wear out. Called ten thousand times, what was placed
in them first is still there.

## Changing It Does Not Remove It

Some people change their name. The meaning does not suit them, the sound
grates, a different name has come to fit the life. Changing it does not make
the first one disappear. The fact that someone made that name and waited with
it does not change. Afterward it remains a thing that happened.

That is what this chapter is looking at. Among the things we receive, there
are some that cannot be given back, and either there is no one left to take
them, or they are not the kind of thing that can be returned.

## What If It Was Not a Good Name

Here is where this gets stuck.

I wrote that a name holds a wish, and for some people that wish is heavy. A
name made so they would become like somebody. A name made to carry on something
in the family. From the receiving end those names are a weight. I could not read
this chapter aloud to such a person as it stands.

Then I should write down where a wish ends and a weight begins, and I tried
several times and deleted it, and the same name is a wish in one house and a
weight in another. What divides them seems to be the sentences that came
after, not the name, and I cannot put those sentences here, so I am leaving
it.

## Someone Comes to Mind

Someone made your name. Have you ever heard why that one. If you have not, it
is worth thinking about whether there is still a person you could ask. The story
is usually short, and short things of that kind stay for a long time.

## Where This Chapter Came From

Nothing in this chapter was brought in from outside. What people look for when
naming a child is written at the range anyone would recognize, with no research
behind it. I considered a passage on how naming differs between countries and
cut it. That direction turns the chapter into a piece about custom, and what it
is looking at is those few months of having made a name and waiting.

The blank in the middle is for the author to fill. Empty, this chapter is a
thing anyone could have written.
