## Counting It Up

Eleven chapters have gone by. Counted up, it comes to this.

Received without permission, and most of it absent from memory. A name made
before arrival. Things bought when there was nobody to use them. A first gift
with no price on it. Carried by somebody over a road walked by no step of your
own. Tens of thousands of repetitions teaching speech, missing from both
memories. A place made ready by someone who got there first. Invisible for as
long as it is still arriving. And it cannot be repaid. So it goes sideways.

Shortened to one line it is one sentence.

> I started out having received.

## What That Sentence Does

It looks obvious. It looks obvious, and once you have believed it, a few things
quietly shift.

The assumption that what I hold was mine to begin with comes loose. Then giving
something up stops feeling like a loss, because it was received in the first
place. And looking at what somebody else holds shifts a little too. The thought
arrives that they also started out having received.

It is not a large change. Nobody becomes a different person overnight. Only, at
every place where you are about to weigh something, there is now one beat. That
one beat is everything this book has to offer.

## And It Does Not End Here

This book ties no knot.

It cannot. Across eleven chapters eleven unresolved places have stacked up, and
the largest of them is still sitting there untouched. Where did what we received
come from.

Say it came from parents and the question follows: who did they receive from.
Step back a generation at a time and the line keeps going backward. At no point
does a place appear where you could say **it began here.** Everyone along it is
somebody who received and handed on.

Where that line began I do not know. That is why the line drawn on the cover
comes in from off the page. The beginning is not inside the picture.

## Toward the Second Book

Looking only at the receiving side filled twelve chapters. And what is received
does not sit still. It goes sideways, in the direction the eleventh chapter
showed.

The second book looks at that side. Going without asking the price. Going where
nothing comes back. Going without weighing whether anyone deserves it. Watch
what people do there and it explains even less than what we saw on the receiving
side.

Set the two covers side by side and the line joins. The line that reached a
single point in the first book sets out again in the second. That point is you.

## Someone Comes to Mind

One person has kept coming to mind while you read this.

You do not have to have said anything to them yet. Having thought of them is
enough for today. They do not know that you did, and without their knowing it,
the thing has already happened.

## Where This Chapter Came From

Nothing in this chapter was brought in from outside. It stands on what the
preceding eleven chapters brought.

The near-absence of outside material in this book was deliberate, and there
were several places a number could have propped something up, and a number
turns the person in that place into a statistic. Where to put one and where
not to was a decision taken each time, and the trace of each decision is at
the end of each chapter.

Five blanks are for the author to fill. The name in the third chapter, the
drawer in the fourth, the back in the sixth, the day in the tenth, and this
one.
