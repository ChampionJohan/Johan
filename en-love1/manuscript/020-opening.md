## Opening

There is one thing to clear away first. **This is not a book about how to love
people well.**

Those books exist and some of them are good. They tend to start in the same
place, though. How do I give so that it works. How do I give so the other person
changes. How do I give so that I feel right about it. All of them are about
giving with a result.

What this book looks at is the other side. **What arrived before I was in a
position to ask for anything.**

A name chosen while I was still unborn. Clothes bought for a person nobody had
met. A back that carried me down a road I cannot remember. None of it was
requested, none of it was paid for, and most of it is not in my memory at all.

That last part is the strange one. **The bulk of what we received is gone from
us.** The years in which a person is made are the years no one remembers, and
the people who did the making mostly do not remember either. Ask them and they
say it was nothing much.

Twelve chapters, and one question follows all twelve.

> What was here before I was?

I will not answer it in this book. I am not sure the answer belongs to me. What
I can do is point at the things, one chapter at a time, and let you look.
