## Did Anyone Consent

Did you ever consent to being born. No. Nobody did, and you were not asked,
you could not have been asked, and had anyone asked you were in no condition
to answer, so the largest thing that happens to a person happens **without
that person's permission.**

Sit with that and it starts to look odd. We take consent for small things. We
sign forms, we tick boxes on terms we have not read, we listen to an
explanation before surgery and then write our name, and yet the event that
comes before all of those has no signature on it anywhere.

## It Was Already Ready

Here is the stranger part. Permission was never given, and still **everything
was prepared.** There were clothes. Several sets, cut to fit a body that had
not arrived, and there was a place to lie down, there was a name, there was a
settled answer to where food would come from and how, and not one of those
things existed because you said so.

So the order runs like this.

> The preparation came first. The person came after.

Not the other way round. Nobody arrived and made demands. It was all in place
back when there was no one to make a demand at all.

## Why Do It With No Order Placed

It is worth stopping here. People prepare things for reasons, and the reasons
are usually that an order came in, or a favor was asked, or there is a cost to
not doing it. The people in that room had none of the three, and more to the
point, **the one who would have placed the order did not yet exist.**

They did it anyway. They had been doing it for months. You can reach for the
word instinct to explain this, and it does explain it, and once it has explained
it there is nothing left. Call it instinct and the hands folding a small shirt
late at night disappear. I would rather not erase those hands.

## You Find Out Later

The one receiving knows none of it at the time. You find out much later, and
the way people find out is nearly always the same. You end up preparing
something for someone else, and in the middle of that you understand that you
were once prepared for in the same way. That is the order it comes in. You have
to stand in the giving place before you can see what you were given.

Until then it was simply there. Like air.

## Is It a Debt Then

This is where I stop in this chapter.

If you received without granting permission, is that a debt. If it is a debt it
has to be repaid, and if it has to be repaid then being born means starting out
owing. Writing that down makes it hard to breathe. But to say it is not a debt
I would have to claim there is a thing in the world you can receive and never
have to pay back, and I have not seen such a thing anywhere else.

The question comes back in the tenth chapter. I do not solve it there either.
One thing can be set down: the ones who did the preparing never asked to be
repaid. Not once.

## Someone Comes to Mind

Someone prepared something for you before you arrived. Do you know who. You do
not have to. Most people do not. What is certain is that the person existed,
because you are here.

## Where This Chapter Came From

Nothing in this chapter was brought in from outside. It is what I have seen and
what I thought while looking at it.

There is probably research on what households buy and spend before a birth. I
considered looking and then stopped. Put a figure in and the chapter turns into
a piece about what people spend on average now. The hands folding clothes that
night disappear into the statistic. Those hands are what the chapter is looking
at.
