## Eleven Chapters of Why It Does Not Work

Read this far and one thing catches the eye. All eleven chapters have been about
**why it does not work.**

Attach no condition and the effect drops. Look at the one doing worse and there
is no return. Do not weigh deserving and it leaks. Give to a stranger and nothing
comes back. Hide your name and even that is gone. Decline to collect and the loss
is locked in. The time cut away never fills in. The thank you does not come.
Feeding is never counted. Take payment and it closes. And why anyone does it, the
eleventh chapter came up one explanation short.

Set out like that, there are eleven reasons not to.

## And They Do It

And people are doing it.

At this hour somebody is going without sleep, somebody is sending something
without a name on it, somebody is laying out breakfast having never been thanked.
Millions of them, for thousands of years. A thing with eleven reasons against it,
done by that many people for that long.

That is the strangest fact in this book. And the most certain one. The
explanation comes up short and **the only thing confirmed is that it is
happening.**

## The Word *Still*

Ask people why they do it and the answers are short. They do not explain. They
start to explain, stop, and say *still.*

Still, somebody has to. Still, what can you do. Still, he is my son. Still,
they are a person. I have heard this many times, and each time I have thought
about what was left out in front of it. All eleven chapters of this book are
inside that *still.* They know nothing is coming back, they know it is a loss,
they know the thank you will not arrive. They know all of it and then
still.

**They are not doing it in ignorance.** That is the weight of the word.

## What Is Left After the Arithmetic

By the arithmetic every chapter in this book is in deficit. And people carry that
deficit for decades.

So it is one of two things. Either those people cannot do arithmetic, or the
arithmetic we use does not fit this. I think it is the second. The people who
have given longest were not bad at arithmetic. Most of them were extremely
practical, and quick with figures in every other part of life. They simply did
not use arithmetic here.

When something continues without the arithmetic working, something other than
arithmetic is holding it up. What that is I did not find in this book.

## And It Does Not Stop

One thing is left at the end.

This book looked only at what happens on the giving side. And the people it looked
at have one more thing in common. **They do not quit.** Something seems to
continue when nothing comes back, when the other person cannot recognize them,
after that person is gone. None of that was looked at here. Look at it here and
this book gets long and slack.

So this book stops. That the arithmetic does not work and people are doing it
anyway is as far as it went. Why they do not quit has not been written down yet.

## Someone Comes to Mind

Someone did a *still* for you.

They will have known all of it. They will have known nothing was coming back, and
that they might never hear a thank you. They did it knowing. They have not
explained it, and they may never.

## Where This Chapter Came From

Nothing in this chapter was brought in from outside. It stands on what the
preceding eleven chapters brought.

The near-absence of outside material here is for the same reason as the first
book. There were several places where material existed and it was left out.
Putting it in makes the person in that place a case study, and the decision
was taken each time and the trace of it is at the end of each chapter.

Two blanks are for the author to fill. The eye in the second chapter and the
person in the ninth.
