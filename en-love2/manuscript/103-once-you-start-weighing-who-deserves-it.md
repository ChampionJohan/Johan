## A Line Forms

The first thing that appears once you start weighing who deserves something is a
line.

Deserving requires comparison, and to work out who has the better claim you
have to set two or more of them side by side, and set side by side, a front
and a back appear. So the instant deserving is weighed, an order is
manufactured among people. That order was not there before. The side that
started weighing put it in.

## A Back of the Line Appears

Where there is a line there is necessarily a back of it. That is the nature of
deserving.

Rank who deserves help and somebody comes out least deserving, and that person
is very often the one who needs it most, because having the weakest claim
usually means having the least, or having nothing to show. **That is how the
person who needs it most ends up standing at the back.**

This is not the work of bad people. It is what came of trying to be fair.
Fairness presumes a line, and as long as there is a line there is a back of it.

## Going Toward the Undeserving

So there is a separate kind of giving that does not weigh deserving.

No line formed, nobody asked who has the better claim, it simply goes to
whoever is in front of you. Nobody asks about deserving where food is being
handed out. There is almost no place where someone serving a meal weighs whether
this person has earned it.

Why food is exempt is curious. With money it gets weighed and with food it gets
weighed less. Probably because food is more urgent, and if people set down the
arithmetic in front of what is urgent, that is close to what this book is
looking at.

## Not Weighing It Costs You

Something has to be written fairly. Not weighing deserving costs you.

It goes to people who have not earned it, it goes to people who are lying, and
some people receive twice and three times. Deserving was invented to stop that,
and remove it and that starts happening again. So *abolish deserving* is not a
sentence this chapter can say.

What can be set down is that weighing also has a price. **The labor of weighing,
the people who go without while it is weighed, and the person at the back.**
That price does not usually make it into the calculation.

## How Far Should It Be Weighed

Here is where this chapter is stuck.

Weigh nothing and the system collapses. Weigh everything and the one who needs
it most is pushed back. There is a line somewhere between, and the line differs
by place and by season. And the person drawing it is usually standing near the
front, so they draw it without knowing what the back of the line is like.

Knowing that, I still cannot draw it. Every time I drew one and looked, it had
come out favoring the side I was standing on.

## Someone Comes to Mind

Someone never asked whether you deserved it. Did you deserve it at the time. You
probably did not. You did not and you received it, and that is why it is still
in your memory.
