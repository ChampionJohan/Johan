## Opening

One thing has to be cleared away before a book about giving. **How to give well
is not in here.**

Those books exist and some are good. They tend to start in the same place. How
do I give so it works. How do I give so the other person changes. How do I give
so that I feel right. All of them are about giving with a result.

What this book looks at is the opposite side. **Going in the direction where
there is no result.** Going where nothing comes back, going without weighing
whether anyone deserves it, going to someone who will not be grateful. By the
arithmetic all of it is a loss, and people are doing it.

Watch that and it does not explain. The side that explains well has already been
covered by other books, so this one will stay on the side that does not. Twelve
chapters, and one question follows all twelve.

> Why go where nothing comes back.

The first book held the front half of that question. What was received cannot be
repaid, so it goes sideways, I wrote. If that is right then giving is passing
something through, and passing something through needs no reason. Only, the
explanation does not seem to cover all of it, which is why this book looks
again.
