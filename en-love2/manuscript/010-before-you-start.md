## This Is the Second

You can read this without having read the first. The first looked at the
receiving side and this one looks at what the received thing does as it moves
on, so reading them in order joins a line. The line is drawn on the covers.

| Book | What it looks at |
|---|---|
| One · The One Who Was Already There | What had arrived before I ever asked |
| **Two · The One Who Never Asked** | Going toward what will not come back |
| Three · The One Who Stayed | What continues even when they do not know |

## Every Chapter Ends With a Box

> ## Someone Comes to Mind

Not something to do. Someone to remember. This book does not tell you to do
anything. It points at what has already happened, and that happened to you, not
to me. So that box is one I cannot fill.

## Every Chapter Leaves One Thing Open

A book about giving especially wants to close neatly. To keep myself from that,
every chapter has one place where I do not have the answer. Warm writing
collapses in exactly one spot: when it pretends the painful part does not hurt.

There are two painful chapters in this one. The second and the ninth. **A book
that does not hurt is not a warm book.**

## And This Book Does Not Finish Either

No knot is tied in the last chapter. None is tied in the third book either.

## How the Facts Are Handled

Each chapter ends with Where This Chapter Came From, holding both what I checked
and what I cut because I could not check it. Anything unverified was cut whole
rather than softened.
