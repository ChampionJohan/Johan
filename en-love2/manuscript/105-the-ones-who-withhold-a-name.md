## What the Last Chapter Could Not Ask

The last chapter said the reasons of people who withhold their name cannot be
asked for. This chapter starts there. Since they cannot be asked, I will only
guess.

## Guess One — To Keep the Receiver Light

The first is to keep the receiver light.

Know the name and you have to repay. Unable to repay, you feel bad. Feeling bad,
you start avoiding them. So hiding the name is easier on the one receiving. What
was given is received intact and no debt appears.

If that is right, hiding a name is **giving a second time.** You give the thing,
and then you clear away the debt attached to the thing. Two gifts in one motion.

## Guess Two — A Name Changes the Shape

The second concerns the giver.

Attach a name and the act becomes that person's act, it becomes *so-and-so did
such-and-such*, and then people begin to appraise it. Did a lot. Did little.
Just like them. The instant appraisal attaches, the act is a different act.

Withhold the name and all of that falls away. The act remains and the person does
not. Some people want that. Why they want it I again do not know, and there
really are people who find it hard to bear having their name attached.

## Guess Three — Because It Is Not Theirs

The third is the one I find most plausible.

Sometimes the giver does not regard the act as their own, and if you think you
are only passing on what you received, there is no reason to sign it, and it
is like a courier not writing their own name on a parcel.

The eleventh chapter of the first book said what we receive goes sideways, and
if that is right, the giver is someone standing in the middle, and people
standing in the middle do not sign. Whether this guess is right I do not know.
Looked at this way, the first two guesses get explained along with it.

## Some Do Give Their Names

It would be unfair to write up only one side. People give with their names
attached, and there is nothing wrong with it.

Attach a name and the next person follows. Watching and learning starts
happening, and it sometimes spreads a long way. If nobody ever gave a name,
nobody would know such a thing existed in the world. So there is a use to
naming, and the hiding side does not have that use.

## Which Is Better

Here is the stuck place.

Hide it and the receiver is light; name it and the next person follows, and both
are good and they cannot be done together and choosing one means discarding the
other.

Different people choose differently, and what they are choosing on they cannot
explain well themselves. I cannot choose either. Only, looking at the ones who
hide it does something strange to me, and that strange feeling is what made me
write the chapter.

## Someone Comes to Mind

Among the things you have received there is one whose giver you will never know.
That person took away your chance to be grateful. Whether that is unkind or kind
I still do not know.

## Where This Chapter Came From

Nothing in this chapter was brought in from outside. All of it is guesswork and
it is marked as guesswork.

Whether anyone has actually surveyed people who give without their names I did
not look up. Even if such a survey exists I doubt they answered. Answering gives
you a name.
