## Where the Eye Goes

Watch where a parent's eye goes in a house with several children and something
odd is happening. It does not go to the one doing well. It goes to **the one it
is not working out for.**

They think longer about the child with poor marks than the one with good ones,
and their body moves first toward the sick child rather than the healthy one. Of
the child doing fine out in the world it is enough to hear news. The one not
doing fine keeps them awake. By the arithmetic this is backwards. Investment goes
more heavily into what is working, and people do the opposite.

## The One Receiving That Look Does Not Know

Stranger still, the one receiving the look does not know it.

The child on the side where it is not working out often feels less loved. The
look is coming toward them more and they feel that anyway. It is because the
look arrives in the shape of worry. Worry is received as nagging, and once it is
received as nagging it does not read as love.

And the child on the side where it is working out feels slighted too. They feel
nobody comes their way. Both feel slighted and the look is on one side.
**Nobody is at fault and all three of them are hurt.**

## The Arithmetic Stands Backwards

There is one thing to set down in this chapter. **The arithmetic of love stands
backwards to the arithmetic of investment.**

Investment puts more into what will return. Love puts more into what will not.
Investment pulls out when the loss grows and love puts in more. Which is why
every attempt to explain love as investment jams at some point, and the point
is always about the same, and it jams in front of the child for whom nothing
is working.

## To the Other Children

One thing has to be stated plainly. Telling the others that the look going one
way does not mean they are loved less is **true, and no comfort at all on the
listening side.**

The one on the side that is doing fine carries that slight for life. And the
slight is hard to say out loud, because saying it makes you the selfish one,
and that is what the well sibling goes through in a house with a sick one, and
the story rarely gets told. I have not yet made anything I could say to them.

## So Should It Be Split Evenly

Here is where this chapter is stuck.

Splitting it evenly looks fair, and split evenly, the side that needs more comes
up short. Give by need and it does not look fair. Give fairly and somebody goes
without. Both are right and both are wrong.

And the parent is not choosing this anyway. The eye goes by itself. What it
means to argue right and wrong about something nobody chooses I do not know, so
the chapter stops here.

## Someone Comes to Mind

In your house the look went more to one side. Were you that side, or the other.
Either way, if what you felt then is still there, the feeling is not wrong.

## Where This Chapter Came From

Nothing in this chapter was brought in from outside.

What the other children go through in a house with a sick sibling has been
studied. I did not use it. Bring that material here and those people become
case studies, and this chapter is not looking at cases but inside that house,
and that the material exists is worth recording, so that anyone who wants more
can find it.

The blank in the middle is for the author to fill.
