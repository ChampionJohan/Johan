🍞 Today's Bread

"The foreigner residing among you must be treated as your native-born. Love them as yourself."
— Leviticus 19:34

It's easy to overlook people who seem different from us. But God sees every person as worth loving and protecting. When we remember how He welcomed us, He gives us courage to stand with the vulnerable. 🤝

👉 Today's step: Reach out with kindness to someone who is new or feels like an outsider.
