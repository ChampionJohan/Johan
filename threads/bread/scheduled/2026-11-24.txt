🍞 Today's Bread

"Make it your ambition to lead a quiet life."
— 1 Thessalonians 4:11

You don't have to change the world. Jesus already did. You are free to simply love people and do your everyday work well. Even the smallest task counts when it's done for Him. 🌿

👉 Today's step: Pick one ordinary task today and do it with care, quietly offering it to God.
