🍞 Today's Bread

"Whoever wants to save their life will lose it, but whoever loses their life for me will find it."
— Matthew 16:25

Following Jesus sometimes means letting go of something good: our comfort, our plans, our free time. What we give up for Him is never wasted. It opens the door to a fuller life of serving others. 🙌

👉 Today's step: Set aside one hour of your free time this week to help someone who needs it.
