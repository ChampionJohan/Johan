🍞 Today's Bread

"Still other seed fell on good soil, where it produced a crop—a hundred, sixty or thirty times what was sown."
— Matthew 13:8

Our words of faith are like seeds. We may never see where they land, and some may seem to fall on hard ground. But God can make even one small invitation grow into a whole garden. 🌱

👉 Today's step: Invite one person to church or share a word of hope with them today.
