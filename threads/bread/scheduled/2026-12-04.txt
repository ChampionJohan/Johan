🍞 Today's Bread

"We are more than conquerors through him who loved us."
— Romans 8:37

Hardship, pain, even persecution cannot separate us from the love of Christ. That truth gives a joy no circumstance can steal. Whatever you face today, His love is greater, and you can live in joy. 😊

👉 Today's step: Write down one hard thing you are facing, then add beside it, "Nothing can separate me from His love."
