🍞 Today's Bread

"Above all else, guard your heart, for everything you do flows from it."
— Proverbs 4:23

What we let into our hearts shapes how we speak and live. Complaining, impatience, and worry creep in easily. But when we keep our eyes on God, our hearts stay steady and our words bring peace. ❤️

👉 Today's step: When you feel a complaint rising today, pause and turn it into a short prayer of thanks.
