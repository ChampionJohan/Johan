🍞 Today's Bread

"Freely you have received; freely give."
— Matthew 10:8

God's grace was never meant to stop with us. The mercy, healing, and hope He has poured into our lives are gifts to pass on. When we give freely, His goodness flows through us to the people who need it most. 🎁

👉 Today's step: Give something away today, whether time, money, or encouragement, to someone in need.
