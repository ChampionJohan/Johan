🍞 Today's Bread

"As the heavens are higher than the earth, so are my ways higher than your ways and my thoughts than your thoughts."
— Isaiah 55:9

God doesn't always answer the way we hope. Like a wise father, He sees what we cannot. When His answers don't make sense, we can still trust His love and His perfect wisdom. ☁️

👉 Today's step: Bring one unanswered prayer to God today and tell Him, "I trust Your way."
