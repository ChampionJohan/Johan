🍞 Today's Bread

"Better a neighbor nearby than a relative far away."
— Proverbs 27:10

In a busy, screen-filled world, many of us barely know the people next door. Yet God designed us for close, caring community. A nearby friend can bring comfort, wise counsel, and point us back to Christ's love. 🏡

👉 Today's step: Learn the name of one neighbor or greet them warmly today.
