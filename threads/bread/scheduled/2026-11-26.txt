🍞 Today's Bread

"He saved us, not because of righteous things we had done, but because of his mercy."
— Titus 3:5

Grace is a gift we never earn. Sometimes God pours it out through a simple meal, a kind word, or an open door. This Thanksgiving, remember how much you have received, and let His grace flow through you to others. 🍽️

👉 Today's step: Invite someone who may be alone to share a meal or a call with you.
