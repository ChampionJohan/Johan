🍞 Today's Bread

"My people have exchanged their glorious God for worthless idols."
— Jeremiah 2:11

We all lean on something: money, people, plans. Even good things can fail us like a leaky filter. Only God never breaks. He is the living water, and our deepest trust belongs to Him alone. 💧

👉 Today's step: Name one thing you have been relying on more than God, and give that worry back to Him in prayer.
