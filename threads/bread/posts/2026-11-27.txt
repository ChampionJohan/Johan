🍞 Today's Bread

"How many are your works, LORD! In wisdom you made them all."
— Psalm 104:24

From the deep sea to the smallest flower, everything God made shows His wisdom and care. The same Maker who designed the world made you, and He keeps caring for all He has made. 🌸

👉 Today's step: Step outside for five minutes, notice one thing God made, and thank Him for it.
