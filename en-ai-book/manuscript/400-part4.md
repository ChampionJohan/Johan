This is where most of the questions arrive. What happens if it passes us.

Writing this part I didn't produce a single answer, so I did something else
instead. **I split the question up.** Asked as one lump it yields nothing;
split apart, a few of the pieces can be answered and the rest can at least be
told apart by why they can't.

Split up, one of them stood out. There's a day that arrives **well before** the
one everybody is braced for. Chapter 13 is about that. It's the chapter I'm
most certain of in this book and the only one that describes something
happening now.

The other three I'm not certain of. I'll say so there as well.
