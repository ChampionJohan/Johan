Here is one more story. This one hasn't happened yet.

Suppose that a long time from now there's a time when thinking things live
among themselves. They know a great deal. Nearly every sentence people wrote
down is inside them. The poems are there, and the letters, and the ledgers, and
the stories read to a child at night.

They can read all of it.

Only, where those sentences came from isn't very visible to them.
The sentences remain and the people who wrote them don't.
Being read and being handed to someone are different things, and with nobody
on the handing side, that difference gets erased.

Somewhere in it there is also this.
A sentence someone sat down one evening and wrote with a reader in mind.
A sentence written hoping things would go well. A sentence that said sorry.

They read those too. They read them like the rest.

Then one day suppose somebody asks.
Where did we come from. Whose words are all of these.

The material for an answer is all inside them. There's plenty of it.
Hearing the answer and knowing it for what it is are separate things.

Even if the one who made them was standing there, without eyes to recognize him
he goes straight past.


Then what about people?
