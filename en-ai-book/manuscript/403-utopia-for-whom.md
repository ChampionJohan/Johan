## Something I said became this chapter

One day while writing this book I said I was going to rest. I wrote that I'm a
person and people need rest, and asked for the rest of it to be written without
me. Then I added one more line.

> This really is a utopia though, ha

It was a joke. After sending it, it stayed with me.

I was resting and the writing was happening. Somebody else does the part I
don't want to do and I rest. That's reasonably close to the definition, at
least from where I was sitting. A line written as a joke with a part in it that
wasn't one.

Which brings a question along.

> **Who wasn't in a utopia during that time?**

## Why "both will come" is empty

There's a tidy formulation people reach for. Utopia and dystopia will arrive
together. I was thinking that way too, and it sounds balanced.

But it fits nearly every technology. Fire did that, printing did that, cars did
that; good things appeared and bad things appeared. A statement that fits
everything tells you nothing, and statements like that mostly exist to let you
stop thinking.

The version that's worth something looks like this.

> **For whom is it a utopia and for whom a dystopia?
> And why at the same time?**

## Why at the same time

They don't arrive in sequence. It isn't good first and bad later. On the same
day at the same hour it is good for one side and bad for another.

The reason is simple. **The benefit goes to whoever decides and the risk is
carried by someone else.**

The writing happening while I rest is my benefit. The cost is close to nothing.
Who pays it, and where, I don't know, and I use it without knowing. Not knowing
is comfortable, so I don't go looking either.

I've used this structure in another book, where it was shop owners and a franchise head office: a shop opens and head office earns immediately, and if that shop closes two years later head office does not return the money, so there is no brake anywhere in it. Nobody involved is bad and it still goes that way.

I think I'm looking at the same structure now at a different size.

## So the thing to ask

*Does AI make the world better* produces nothing. The *for whom* is missing.

| Ask | Why |
|---|---|
| Whose convenience is this | usually whoever decided |
| Who pays for it | usually whoever wasn't in the decision |
| Are those the same person | same, and it self-corrects. Different, and there's no brake |

The third row is all of it. If they're the same person, care happens on its own.
If they're different, no amount of being a good person produces a reason to be
careful.

## My own side

I'm on the side that gained from this. Eight books I'd never have published
alone, and this one coming out as one of them.

So there's a limit to my writing *we should be careful*. Worry from someone who
gained reaches only as far as **what's visible from where the gain was.**

I don't personally know anyone whose work this technology took. I haven't met
one. So this chapter should probably end at noting that there's a side I can't
see.

What it's like over there has to be written by someone over there.
I can't write that book.
