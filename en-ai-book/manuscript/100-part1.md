This book starts with what happened to me.

I published eight books with an AI. Part One is what I saw while doing that.
The questions come afterwards.

There's a reason for that order. Most of what gets written about AI is written
from outside it — by people who haven't used it, or used it twice, or are
selling something. I ran a business on it. Eight times. Some of it worked and
some of it went wrong, and this part is the record.

A few chapters in you'll notice this isn't a book about technology.
