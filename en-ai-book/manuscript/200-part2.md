Part One was what I saw. From here on it's what I asked.

Putting the questions in order, I decided this one had to come first: what is
it made of. It belongs ahead of how far the performance goes, because if you
don't know what went into a thing, the worries that follow tend to point in
the wrong direction entirely.

All four chapters here look the same way. Not inside the thing, but at
**what went in and came out as that.**

And it's worth saying in advance that none of the four produces a clean answer.
