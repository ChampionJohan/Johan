In Part Two I asked what the thing was made of, and the answer was mostly
*human stuff*. Which brings the next question along by itself: is there
anything left between a person and that.

I expected this to be the hard part. It was the reverse. The hard part wasn't
finding a difference, it was finding **a difference that doesn't shrink with
time.** Split it by what each can do and the list gets shorter every year; the
things written down today as human-only get crossed off one at a time.

So what Part Three looks for isn't the difference now but the one that doesn't
wear. Three chapters, and the last is probably the one that stays correct
longest, because what's written in it is physics.
