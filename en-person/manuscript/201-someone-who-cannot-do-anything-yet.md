## That Room

The day the child was born I was at the hospital and the family had come. Nobody
in that room asked the child anything. There was nothing to ask. The child had
done nothing that day and would not do anything for some time. And people had
gathered.

What they did, having gathered, was look. That was all of it.

## Make a List of What They Can Do

Write down what a newborn cannot do and the list is long. Cannot walk, cannot
speak, cannot even eat without help. Holding the head up takes months, and the
day they roll over the household makes a noise about it, and the reason for the noise is exactly that: it means there is that little they can do. Make a list of
what they can do and the list is nearly empty, and beside that empty list the
people in the room put that person at the center.

**In that room the order is inverted.** The one who can do least is furthest in.

## Nobody Finds That Strange

It is not like that elsewhere. In an office the ones who can do things are at the
center, and in a classroom too. The person who talks most in a meeting is usually
the person who can do most, and when someone who can do nothing comes in, the room
gets tighter. I am not saying that is bad. In a place where work is done, turning
that way is natural, and if it did not turn that way the work would not get done.

But in that room it turns the other way. And they are the same people. Someone
who spent the afternoon going over a subordinate's numbers walks into that room in
the evening and gives up the center to the one who can do nothing. Nobody ordered
it and nobody taught it; it switches by itself at the threshold.

So this can be said. **We know how to do both.** We know how to count people by
usefulness and we know how to not count them, and it is not that we do not know how to stop. We do it in some places and not in others.

## One Answer to Clear Out of the Way First

There is an explanation that usually comes up here. We are investing, holding
something back because it will be useful later. In that case the baby is an
investment.

But nobody in that room behaves that way. Nobody holds a child while calculating
what the child will do for them later. If someone did, we would spot it at once.
When a person starts talking that way the room goes quiet. And when it is certain
that the child will never be useful later, that room stays the same. You can see
it beside a child born ill — the arithmetic has hit the floor and the seat at the
center has not moved.

So the explanation is wrong. Investment does not explain that room.

## Then Why Only There

This is where I cannot answer.

With a baby, stopping the count is easy. Nobody taught us and everybody does it,
including with a stranger's baby. But the same thing does not work with adults.
In front of someone long ill, someone out of work, someone old, the stopping does not come by itself; it takes effort, and the fact that it takes effort already
means it is different. Both of them can do nothing, and it only works on one side.

**I do not know this one.** There seems to be something between a person who has
not done anything yet and a person who can no longer do it, and I have thought
about what that something is for a long time without getting my hand on it. A few
explanations come up. There is still time ahead. It is my own blood. It is small.
Not one of them stretches as far as a stranger's baby.

So I stop here and go to the next chapter. The other three chapters of Part Two
are about the people who can no longer do it.

## Today

Take one person you met today and look at them as someone doing nothing. That
means erasing, for a moment, what they do. Fold away what they accomplished
today, what they earn, what position they hold, and look at them.

It will feel strange. The strangeness is what this book is about. One person is
enough, and it does not have to last long.

## Where This Chapter Comes From

There are no outside facts in this chapter. No statistics, no studies, no other
people's books. That is true of all four chapters in Part Two. What is here is
what I saw and what I thought while standing there. There is a reason for that:
what this part is trying to do is not to prove something but to make visible
something already being done, and material cannot do that.

One thing to note instead. Clearing away the "useful later" explanation uses the case of a child born ill, and that is written as a thing commonly known rather than as
a case, and it is not anyone's particular household.
