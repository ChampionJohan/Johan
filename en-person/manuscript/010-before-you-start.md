## What This Book Is Doing

In one line:

> **How to see a person with usefulness taken away.**

It does not argue that a person has worth. An argument can be answered, and an
argument written so that it cannot be answered is where a reader stops thinking.
So this book took another road. It goes to the places where usefulness really
does fall to zero and watches what people do there. What people do cannot be
argued with. It is simply there.

## Five Parts

| Part | What it looks at |
|---|---|
| One · When the Counting Started | The day a number got attached to a person, and what numbers do |
| Two · Take It Away and Look | Four places where usefulness is zero. No outside material in this part |
| Three · So How Do You Look | Four things to do |
| Four · Between Two People | Where those four fail hardest — home, work, the street |
| Five · After the Machines | Where competing on usefulness is a contest already decided |

## Two Things Are Attached to Every Chapter

**One is "Today."** Every chapter has one, except the last. Read only, and this
book becomes a sermon. Do them, and it becomes a book. All sixteen are small.
Not one of them takes more than a day.

**One is a place left unresolved.** Every chapter has one of those too. A
question I do not have an answer to, written down as it is. It is not an
oversight. If I could have written seventeen chapters that close cleanly, that
would have been a different book.

## How the Facts Are Handled

Every chapter ends with a section called **Where This Chapter Comes From.** Two
things go in it: what was checked, and what was cut because it could not be.

Books rarely print the second, and the second is more useful. In some chapters
what was cut runs longer than what was kept. The level of checking is written
down too. **The checking in this book is public sources cross-read against one
another. It is not primary sources.** Where an original could not be opened, it
says so.

Nothing was softened. When something could not be confirmed, it was cut whole
rather than hedged. A hedged sentence gets read as a fact.

## There Is No Closing Chapter

Said here in advance. **This book has no closing chapter.** The last chapter
ends on a question and nothing is written after it.

That is not a mistake. Anything put in that space turns into a sermon from the
first line. Stopping there is the last thing this book can do.
