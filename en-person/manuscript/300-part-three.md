Up to here it was looking. From Part Three it is doing.

Four chapters and four things. Saying a person's name. Listening to the end.
Not asking first what someone does for a living. Not trying to fix it. All four
are small. Not one takes more than a day.

Before starting, something has to be said. **A book of things to do already
exists.** It came out in 1936 and it still sells, and it lays out how to deal with people point by point and the points are mostly right.

So the first chapter of this part starts from that book. Not to refute it. To
see whether the same actions can be recommended **with a different reason
underneath.** Whether same action plus different reason makes a different book
is a question I ended up asking myself, and it is in chapter eight exactly as I
asked it.

Said in advance: not one of these four chapters closes cleanly. All four are
things worth doing, and all four hit a wall at the end on the question of how
far. The wall is left standing where it is.
