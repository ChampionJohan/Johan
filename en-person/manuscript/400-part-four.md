The four things in Part Three mostly work in front of a stranger. Part Four is
the places where they do not.

Four chapters. Home, work, the mouth, the street. In order of closeness.

The first is the closest place and the place where it fails hardest. That is
backwards, and there is a reason it is backwards. Care produces expectation,
and expectation brings a ruler with it. So the person we love most is the person
we measure first.

The second chapter is outside the house. There the same thing has been built
into a procedure, and the person doing the measuring does not feel bad about it.
It is their job.

The third has no paper in it. It is reducing a person to a single word, and
because there is no document there is nowhere to ask for a correction. Of the
three it is the most common.

The fourth is strangers. The people we pass in a day we know only as roles, and this part ends on the finding that they are not nothing to us, and Part Five
opens from there.
