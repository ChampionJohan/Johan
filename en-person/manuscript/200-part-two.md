Part One was about measuring. Part Two goes to the places where there is nothing
to measure.

Four people appear. Someone who cannot do anything yet. Someone stopped for a
while. Someone handing back, one at a time, what they used to be able to do.
Someone skilled with nowhere left to use it. All four are at or near zero.

**There is not one piece of outside material in this part.** No papers, no
statistics, no history. Only what I saw.

That was done on purpose. Bring material into these four places and the person
standing there becomes a case, and once they are a case, what this book is trying
to do stops right there. Part One was about the devices that turn people into
cases. Doing the same thing in Part Two would be the book breaking its own word.

So this part runs without numbers. What you should be left with after four
chapters is one thing. **What people do in those four places resembles itself.**
Part Three starts from the resemblance.
