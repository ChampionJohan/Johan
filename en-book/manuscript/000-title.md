## Who's Actually Paying?

### Take Any Business Apart in Five Questions

**The Five Boxes Series · Book One**

---

This book breaks any business into five questions.

> **1. Customer** — Who actually pays?
> **2. Value** — What are they paying for?
> **3. Payment** — How, and when, does the money arrive?
> **4. Moat** — Why hasn't anyone taken it yet?
> **5. Fault Line** — Where does it break?

Part One builds the five boxes one at a time.
Part Two runs eighteen businesses across nine fields through all five.

By the end you'll be able to read **companies that aren't in this book.**

That is the only thing this book is for.
