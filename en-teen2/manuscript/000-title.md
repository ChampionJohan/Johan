## I Started. So Why Isn't It Working?

### The Part Nobody Warns You About

**The Five Boxes Series · Teen Edition, Book Two**

---

Book One ended like this.

> Show it to exactly one person before the day is over.
> They can buy it or not buy it. Starting is the thing that counts.

So if you started, by now you've hit a wall.

Nobody bought it. Or one person bought it and that was the end of it.
Or you did the math and there was nothing left over.

**This book is about that wall.**

Book One was a book about getting started.
This one is a book about **not quitting.**
