## The Fourth Box

### Why Some Businesses Can't Be Taken

**The Five Boxes Series · Book Two**

---

Book One broke a business into five questions.

> **1. Customer** — Who actually pays?
> **2. Value** — What are they paying for?
> **3. Payment** — How, and when, does the money arrive?
> **4. Moat** — Why hasn't anyone taken it yet?
> **5. Fault Line** — Where does it break?

The first three ask *how it earns right now.*

**This book digs into the last two only.** The boxes that ask
*whether it gets to keep earning.*

If Book One was a photograph, this is video.

By the end you'll be able to work out **how long a company is likely to last.**
