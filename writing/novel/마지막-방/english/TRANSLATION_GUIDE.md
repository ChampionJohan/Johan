# The Last Room — Translation Guide

Book 7 of the **Loop Chronicles**, and the finale of the series.
Korean source: 『마지막 방』. This guide is authoritative. Where it
conflicts with a translator's instinct, the guide wins — consistency
across 48 chapters matters more than any single elegant line.

Books 1–6 use the same conventions; see their guides under
`../../루프-연대기/english/`, `../../울음-장부/english/`,
`../../사십일-조항/english/`, `../../이삭-줍는-자리/english/`,
`../../마른-골짜기/english/`, `../../저자-없는-책/english/`.

## 4. Register and voice

- **First-person retrospective.** Mone narrates after the fact and knows
  how it ends. Past tense throughout.
- **Italic passages** marked `*...*` are her later commentary. Keep them
  italic. **Arche's speech is also italic** — context separates them, as
  it does in the Korean; do not add quotation marks to Arche's lines.
- **Dialogue opens with an ellipsis**: `"…네."` → `"…Yes."` Keep the
  ellipsis exactly.
- **Mone puts things in order.** That is her characterizing habit, as
  counting was Anemos's (Bk 5) and reading to the last line was Hora's
  (Bk 6). Wherever the Korean names a sequence, a count, or a position,
  keep it exact. Her ordering habit — not cleverness — is what makes the
  ch.23–24 date alignment possible, and the English must not make her
  sound clever.
- **Short paragraphs, spare prose.** Resist smoothing the clipped
  sentences into flowing English periods.
- **No Scripture epigraphs** per chapter. Each chapter opens with a
  `> *이전 이야기*` recap block only → render as `> _Previously_ — ...`
- When a record is quoted with a chapter citation (e.g. *안식소 기록 2부
  4장*), render it as **the Haven record, Part Two, Chapter 4** and keep
  the citation — that is Arche indexing, and it is in-world.

## 5. Scripture

Use the **King James Version** only (public domain) — but note that in
this book **Scripture is never quoted at all.** Three texts underlie it
and must stay audible only as diction:

**Daniel 12:4** — *"But thou, O Daniel, shut up the words, and seal the
book, even to the time of the end: many shall run to and fro, and
knowledge shall be increased."*

**John 14:2** — *"In my Father's house are many mansions: if it were not
so, I would have told you."*

**John 19:30** — *"It is finished."*

Three hard consequences:

1. **The seal in ch.36 reads 「마지막 때까지」 → "UNTIL THE TIME OF THE
   END."** That phrase is the only place the Daniel diction surfaces, and
   it appears as words on a seal, not as a quotation.
2. **The margin line in ch.35 is a carpenter's note, not a verse.**
   『방은 넉넉하게 남겨 둔다 — 누가 더 올지 몰라서』 →
   **"Leave the rooms generous — you never know how many more will
   come."** Keep it practical and slightly ungrammatical, the way a
   builder writes a reminder to himself. **Never make it sound biblical.**
3. **Chapter 47's "…다 됐어." → "…That's it, then."** — see §6. It must
   NOT be "It is finished." The whole series has withheld that line, and
   the English must withhold it too.

Histor's **"What changes if you open it?"** in chapter 39 must use the
same construction in English. The rhyme is deliberate and it is the spine
of the book.

## 8. File format

Output filenames: `NN-kebab-case-title.md`, e.g. `01-the-apprentice.md`,
`24-the-same-day.md`, `48-the-last-room.md`.

Each file:

```
---
title: Chapter N. English Title
series: The Last Room, Part One · In Progress
---

# Chapter N. English Title

> _Previously_ — two to four lines translated from the Korean
> `> *이전 이야기*` block.

Body, preserving every `---` scene break and every *italic* passage.

*(Continued in Chapter N+1.)*
```

End markers:
- Chapters 1–11, 13–23, 25–35, 37–47 → `*(Continued in Chapter N+1.)*`
- Chapter 12 → `*(Continued in Part Two.)*`
- Chapter 24 → `*(Continued in Part Three.)*`
- Chapter 36 → `*(Continued in Part Four.)*`
- Chapter 48 → `*(The End.)*`

## 9. Rules that must not be broken

1. **Arche never lies and never hides anything.** Not once, to the very
   end. What it cannot do is **close** a case — and that is an undefined
   condition, never concealment. Never translate *등재되어 있지 않습니다*
   as though something were withheld.
2. **Loop is a good city** and Mone means it. No irony, no menace the
   Korean does not earn. Arche is never defeated, shut down, or made to
   repent, and the world does not end.
3. **Histor is the person in this book who knows the most.** He never
   mocks, handles the records with real care, and is **never refuted.**
   In ch.18 his objection is largely correct; in ch.19 he concedes a fact
   and does not change his position. **He never believes,** including in
   ch.45. Do not soften him toward belief anywhere.
4. **Nobody changes position.** Every returning character in Part Three
   is exactly where their own book left them — Zeteo still unbelieving,
   Lytron still never having lost anything, Eikon still lacking nothing
   and still un-released, Tyche still calling it coincidence. There is
   no conversion scene in this book. Do not add warmth that implies one.
5. **The finale explains nothing.** Never let the English resolve what
   the Korean leaves open: **nobody says who he is**; the two records
   placing him in two cities on the same day are **never explained**;
   the plan has **no designer's name** and nobody supplies one; and the
   name of the man walking ahead at the very end is **never given.**
6. **No Scripture is quoted anywhere.** Not Daniel, not John, not
   Revelation. The series has never quoted a verse in the body and must
   not begin in its final chapter. In particular, ch.47's line is
   **"…That's it, then."** — never "It is finished."
7. **Never gloss the names before chapter 44.** In ch.44, spoken by Mena:
   **Mone = μονή, "an abiding place," a room to stay in — the same root
   as Mena's own name (μένω, "to abide").** **Histor = ἵστωρ, "one who
   has found out," the root of the word *history*.** Histor laughs at his
   own and then has nothing to say; preserve that silence.
8. **Chapter 47 must stay flat.** He has been folding paper for weeks and
   nobody can say when he arrived. When he says his line, **nobody
   reacts** — the Keeper answers "…Thank you for your work," Mena starts
   talking about dinner. **No recognition, no awe, no hush.** Only Mone
   writes it down, and she does not explain why.
9. **Chapter 48's empty box.** Mone leaves the recipient's name blank.
   In Book 6 the blank was one **nobody had filled**; this one is
   **not yet filled.** The Korean states the difference exists and
   refuses to explain it. The English must do the same.
10. **The narrator never speaks of volumes, series, readers, or craft.**
    The other books are **city records** ("the Haven record"), never
    "Book Two." Mone addresses "이 기록을 읽는 사람" — **the one reading
    this record** — never "the reader."
