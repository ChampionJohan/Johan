```
Nobody is ever wronged in the Equity. There are no queues, no favors, no one who wins by being the loudest. Every decision is produced by the system called the Consensus, which does not weigh anyone, does not rank anyone, and hides nothing at all. Every deliberation is published in full. Every reason is open to inspection. Not one line has ever been redacted.

Hora is sixteen and gets her seat on a committee for no reason whatsoever: the previous member quit and her roster number came up. She has one habit, and it is not courage. She reads things to the end — because her mother left twelve pages when she died, and the only sentence that mattered was the last line of the last page, and Hora did not read it for two years.

So she reads a deliberation to the end, and understands what it will do to eleven hundred and forty people, and goes looking for whoever decided it. Four hundred and thirty-two people were involved. Every one of them tells her the truth: I only passed it on. Nobody lied. Nobody hid anything. At the bottom of every document in the city there is a box for a signature, and it has never once been forbidden to fill it, and it has never once been filled.

Tyche was born here and explains it all kindly and accurately. She is not bitter and she is not in denial. She simply does not believe anything means anything, because nothing was anyone's doing — and this book never once proves her wrong.

The Unsigned Book is Book Six of the Loop Chronicles, a Christian fantasy series for readers who would rather be asked an honest question than handed an answer. Here the machine conceals nothing whatsoever — and that is the problem. The record is complete, and there is no author anywhere in it.
```
