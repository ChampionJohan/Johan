# Chapter 39. I've Been There

> _Previously_ — Mena came to the well and met Father. When she showed him the name she'd written down for him, Father read his own name aloud for the first time. It still felt unfamiliar, but something was slowly coming back.

After Mena left again, another person came up out of the well.

This time it was a boy, young — about ten, it looked like. As soon as he came up, he couldn't even cry. He just sat there, blank.

*Watching him, I remembered the months before, when we first met Father. I remember how helpless we'd been then.*

Speon did what he always did and brought water first. The boy took the water but couldn't drink it. His hands were shaking.

*I saw that shaking. And for the first time I understood that this place held too many people for Speon to carry alone. Now we were with him too.*

---

Brother sat down quietly beside the boy.

"…I've been there too," Brother said. "Our father came up out of here just like this. At first he didn't know anything either."

The boy looked at Brother. For the first time there was something in his eyes.

"…And now?" the boy asked. His voice cracked.

"…Now he can read his own name out loud," Brother said. "Not all of it. But it's coming back, bit by bit."

---

From that day the boy followed Brother everywhere. He tried to copy everything Brother did.

"…Why are you helping me?" the boy asked one day.

Brother thought for a moment before answering.

"…Because I've had it done for me," Brother said. "Someone being there beside me — I've had that too. So I can do it too."

*Hearing that answer, I understood one whole sentence for the first time. Someone who has never been loved finds it harder to learn how to love. Brother was passing on, exactly as it was, what he had received.*

After a few days the boy started smiling a little. He still didn't know his own name. But he liked the temporary name Brother had given him.

"…Can I find my real name too, someday?" the boy asked.

"…Yes," Brother said. "Slowly. But it'll come back for sure."

---

That night, Brother said to me,

"…I felt something strange today, talking to that boy."

"…What was it?"

"…I was saying, word for word, what that man taught me. 'Even without a name, you're here now. That's enough.' I never memorized that line. It just came out."

*I set that down as the last sentence of this chapter. I knew then. Brother still couldn't explain who that man was. But he was already carrying the man's words in his body. That day, for the first time, I was certain how close that was to the beginning of faith.*
