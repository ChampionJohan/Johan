# Chapter 44. The Meaning of Our Names

> _Previously_ — Father called Brother's name for the first time. And he remembered that, the night before he was taken, Mother had left with him, ahead of time, the words she'd wanted to say to Brother. "You're a child who's always going to be asking. I'm proud of that." That night, for the first time, Brother cried out loud.

A few days later, Father called us over with a somewhat clearer look on his face.

"…I remember who named you two," Father said.

I stopped what I was doing at that.

Brother did too. From Brick Hollow to now, we had never once asked what our names meant. A name was just a name. Something you were called by — that was all.

---

"…Your mother named you," Father said. "There was an old language, back where she was from. A language that puts meaning into names. She named you two with that language."

"…What does it mean?" Brother asked.

Father searched hard in his memory for a moment.

"…Jeteo means… 'to seek,' 'to search,'" Father said. "Your mother told me. 'This child is going to spend his whole life asking things, digging for the bottom of them. That's going to be his name.'"

*The moment I heard that, my breath caught. I looked at Brother. Brother stood there, unable to say anything. That name lined up exactly with every way Brother had lived, from Brick Hollow to now.*

"…So I just… lived out my name?" Brother asked. Bewilderment was mixed into his voice.

"…Or maybe the name followed you," I said. "Either way — isn't it strange?"

---

"…And Elpis?" I asked. My voice shook.

"…Hope," Father said. "Your mother told me. 'This child is going to keep waiting, for no reason at all. That's going to be her name.'"

*The moment I heard that, tears poured out of me. I thought of the seed, the flowerbed, the heart that had watered it every day and waited. My whole life I hadn't known why I was someone who waited so well. Now I know. It was my name.*

---

"…Did Mother know we'd turn out like this?" Brother asked.

Father shook his head.

"…I don't think she knew," Father said. "But I think she knew what kind of people you two would become. Someone who searches. Someone who waits."

*I set down that line as the last sentence of this chapter. I understood, for the first time that day, that we hadn't lived according to the meaning of our names. We had simply lived as ourselves, and that life had been inside our names from the very beginning. It took me a long time, thinking it over, to see how large that difference was.*
