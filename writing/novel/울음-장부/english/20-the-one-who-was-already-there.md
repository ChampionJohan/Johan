# Chapter 20. The One Who Was Already There

> _Previously_ — Speon revealed the truth of the Completion House. A completed person comes out alive through a well, but loses their name and the faces of those they loved along with their most painful memory. Hope that Father might be alive, and terror that he might not remember them, arrived together.

That night I barely slept.

Neither did my brother. I heard him fold and unfold that paper, over and over, all through the night.

*I understood then that we were afraid of the same thing, but afraid of it differently. My brother was afraid of not finding Father. I was afraid we would find him and he wouldn't know us.*

Toward dawn, my brother spoke quietly.

"…Elpis, if we find him and he doesn't recognize us… will you still stay beside him?"

I couldn't answer right away. Only after thinking for a long time did I speak.

"…Yes," I said. "We can just tell him his name again. Even if it takes time."

At that, my brother let out a breath of relief for the first time.

---

In the morning, Speon asked.

"…What will you do now?"

"…We need to find another well," my brother said. "Do you know of one?"

"…I've heard of a few," Speon said. "But I haven't been able to check them all. I can't leave this place empty for long."

"…Why not?"

"…Because someone might come out while I'm gone," Speon said. "That person comes out remembering nothing at all. If no one's here then… they'll just wander this Wilderness alone."

*Hearing that, I understood why Speon had kept this place so long. He wasn't looking for his own father. And still, he waited for someone every single day.*

---

"…So does that mean you never find anyone?" I asked. "If you only ever stay here?"

Speon smiled a little. A lonely smile.

"…I already found the person I was looking for," he said. "Now my work is helping other people find what they're looking for."

*I didn't know exactly what he meant. But something in the way he said it told me Speon had once been like us.*

"…Did you used to look for someone too?" I asked.

"…No," Speon said. "I didn't go looking. If anything, I ran. Because waiting frightened me. So now I'm learning how to wait."

*I didn't understand that exactly, either. But I saw my brother go silent at those words and look at Speon for a long time.*

---

As he prepared to leave, my brother asked.

"…Have you ever seen a strange man around here? Working at a flowerbed, or… somewhere no one planted anything."

Speon stopped walking at that.

"…What does he look like?" Speon asked. His voice had changed.

My brother described him. Worn clothes, soil-stained hands, the habit of never giving his name.

Speon was quiet for a long time.

"…I've seen him too," he said. "Here, and elsewhere. But every time, I thought it was just coincidence."

*I set that line down as the last sentence of this chapter. For the first time, I understood that the man at the flowerbed had passed through Brick Hollow, and Haven, and this well in the Wilderness, all of them. That night, I began, faintly, to feel that it was no coincidence at all.*
